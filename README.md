# Madadgar Balochistan - app downloads

**Madadgar Balochistan** is a free community app that brings together weather, official and satellite-based alerts, floods,
earthquakes, farming tools, jobs, education and health help for Balochistan, in English and Urdu.

This repository only hosts downloadable builds. The app is also available on Google Play.

## Installing the APK by hand

1. Download the `.apk` file to your phone.
2. Open it and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when Android asks.
3. Open **Madadgar Balochistan** and choose your area.

To check the file, compare its SHA-256 with `SHA256SUMS.txt`.

**Note:** an APK installed this way is signed differently from the Google Play version. Android will not install one over the
other, and a hand-installed copy will not receive Play updates. Pick one: install from Google Play, or from this page.

## Important

Madadgar shows information from official and satellite sources. It is **not a government service**, and satellite detections
(for example heat sources) are not confirmed events. Always follow official warnings and emergency instructions.
